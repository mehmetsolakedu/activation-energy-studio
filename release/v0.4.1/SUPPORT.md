# Support and bug reports

Send a reproducible bug report to the maintainer:

<mailto:mehmetsolak@siirt.edu.tr?subject=Activation%20Energy%20Studio%20v0.4.1%20bug%20report>

Use the subject prefix `[SCIENTIFIC]`, `[ACCESSIBILITY]`, `[LANGUAGE]`, or
`[OFFLINE]` when applicable.

Do not include confidential, proprietary, embargoed, or personally identifying
experimental data. A reproducible report should include:

1. Activation Energy Studio version and the SHA-256 of the HTML file.
2. Operating system, browser name and version, and simple/expert mode.
3. Import format, selected sheet/header/layout, units, heating rates, stage,
   alpha range, and method.
4. Visible diagnostic codes and messages.
5. Expected versus observed behavior and reproduction steps.
6. A minimal non-confidential input or one of the bundled example/template names.
7. Exported JSON when safe to share (with sensitive metadata removed).

Security-sensitive reports should begin with `[SECURITY]` and omit exploit
details from the initial message.
