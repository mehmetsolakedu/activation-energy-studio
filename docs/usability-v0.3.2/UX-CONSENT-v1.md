# UX-CONSENT-v1 — Recording Consent Requirements

Before recording, explain that participation is voluntary, withdrawal is
allowed without penalty, the interface rather than the participant is being
tested, and separate screen and audio recordings will be retained for evidence
audit. Only supplied synthetic data may be used.

The signed identity-bearing consent form stays in the coordinator's protected
consent store. The de-identified study record contains only a unique reference
such as `CONSENT-P01-<local-record-id>`. Do not place names, signatures, email
addresses, or contact information in the evidence package.

Required consent-store fields are participant identity, pseudonymous ID,
consent version, UTC signature time, recording permission, retention notice,
withdrawal contact/process, and coordinator countersignature.
