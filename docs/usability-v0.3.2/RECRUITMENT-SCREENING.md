# v0.3.2 Recruitment Screening

Record screening outside the de-identified evidence package, then transfer only
the eligibility booleans and TGA-experience category to the participant record.

Include only people who:

- work or study in a quantitative field;
- can open CSV/XLSX files and read basic plots/tables;
- report `none` or `basic` TGA familiarity;
- have not routinely calculated FWO, KAS, Starink, Friedman, or Kissinger
  results during the preceding two years;
- did not contribute to the product, protocol, or acceptance criteria;
- can read standard technical English; and
- have not seen another participant's session.

Exclude kinetic-method developers, product contributors, and anyone declining
recording consent. The final retained cohort requires exactly five people, at
least two with `basic` TGA familiarity and at least two with `none`.
